One partnership, end to end.

How I'd run a single TechBBQ partnership from research to renewal, following the six responsibilities in the role. Then my first 90 days.

An illustrative exercise. The case is a Türkiye startup pavilion at TechBBQ 2027, pitched to Invest in Türkiye. Contact names, results and figures are hypothetical. Package prices follow TechBBQ's 2025 international price list.

1. Outreach.

Researching a prospect and turning a cold contact into a meeting.

Research before writing: Invest in Türkiye already runs pavilions at Web Summit and VivaTech, and Turkish agencies took startups to Slush in Helsinki. So the hook is simple: you already come to the Nordics, add Copenhagen.

2. Proposal.

Shaping a package around the partner's goals, not our price list.

Partner goals (from the discovery call): show Türkiye as a tech investment destination, get Turkish startups in front of Nordic investors, and reach Nordic corporates.

Partner goalWhat TechBBQ delivers
Visibility as an investment destination42 sqm pavilion in the main hall; branding on all TechBBQ channels; co-host of the Diplomatic Soirée
Startups meet Nordic investors15 startups; 1 grill session with a curated investor group; pre-booked 1:1 meetings in the event app
Reach Nordic corporates1 event room for a Nordic–Türkiye Bridge side event; guided tour for the delegation
Bring the delegation70 TechBBQ passes; 4 partner dinner seats

Investment: Conqueror package, €37,000 excl. VAT (2025 list price). Optional pop-up event: €4,000 after the 20% booth-partner discount (list price €5,000).

Why it's worth it: at Slush and Web Summit, Türkiye competes with thousands of startups. At TechBBQ a 15-startup pavilion is one of a few national programmes, so each startup gets more investor attention per euro.

3. Commercial cycle.

Running meetings and negotiating with a plan, alongside the senior commercial team.

Pitch meeting agenda (30 minutes)

  1. Their 2027 priorities and events calendar (10 min, they talk)
  2. TechBBQ's audience and the Nordic–India model as proof (5 min)
  3. Walk through the pavilion proposal (10 min)
  4. Agree the next step: budget owner and decision date (5 min)

Walk-away line: no discount without a smaller package, and no exclusivity on the Bridge format. Contract: terms sent through GetAccept after review with the senior commercial team.
