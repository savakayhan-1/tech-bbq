Executive summary

The proposal on one page

Objective

Put 15–20 Turkish startups in front of Nordic and international investors, and position Türkiye as a tech partner for the region.

Solution

A half-day Nordic–Türkiye Startup Bridge inside TechBBQ 2027: pavilion, stage session, investor grill sessions and an investor dinner.

Investment

Recommended package from €37,000, with add-ons for co-funders such as technoparks and development agencies.

Outcome

Pre-booked investor meetings, a visible national presence, and an ROI report in October that sets up a multi-year partnership.

Prices based on TechBBQ’s 2025 international packages.

Why now

Türkiye already invests in Europe’s tech stages. The Nordics are next.

  • Türkiye pavilions already run at Web Summit and VivaTech.
  • Development agencies take 100+ Turkish startups a year to Slush, Web Summit and CES.
  • Slush shows Turkish startups want the Nordic market.

TechBBQ is the natural first Nordic stop: two months before Slush, with Copenhagen’s investors, corporates and Danish public partners in one place.

Sources: Invest in Türkiye, BEBKA (Slush 2025), Startups Magazine (London Tech Week 2026).

The programme

The Nordic–Türkiye Startup Bridge: one half-day, four parts

Part 1

Türkiye pavilion

15–20 selected startups in the main hall: AI, fintech, gaming, climate and health.

Part 2

Bridge session

Turkish and Nordic VCs on a TechBBQ stage, discussing cross-border investment.

Part 3

Grill sessions

Each startup pitches to a curated investor group, with 1:1 meetings pre-booked in the event app.

Part 4

Diplomatic Soirée

An investor dinner co-hosted with the Embassy of Türkiye in Copenhagen.

Built on the model of TechBBQ’s Nordic–India and Nordic–Africa summits.

What’s included

Everything the delegation needs, before, during and after

Pavilion space

A branded Türkiye area in the main hall for 15–20 startups.

Stage slot

The Bridge session with Turkish and Nordic investors.

Curated investor access

Grill sessions and 1:1 meetings pre-booked in the event app.

Brand visibility

Partner presence on the TechBBQ website, app and stage.

Startup selection support

Shared criteria so the startups match what Nordic investors look for.

Post-event ROI report

Meetings, leads, attendance and feedback, delivered in October.

Final deliverables are agreed in a scoping workshop before contract.

Activations

Three ways to make the Türkiye presence memorable

Kahve & Capital lounge

A Turkish coffee lounge next to the investor area, with pre-booked 20-minute founder–investor meetings.

Goal: brand presence and qualified meetings

Market entry clinic

A short stage session and office hours on Start-up Denmark and the Türkiye Tech Visa, with the Danish Business Authority.

Goal: show Türkiye is easy to do business with

Bridge challenge

A pitch challenge for Nordic–Turkish startups. The winner gets a trip to an Istanbul tech week.

Goal: a story to share before and after the event

Each activation can be added to the lead package or taken by a co-funder.
