SWOT, PESTEL and the competition.

Three lenses on TechBBQ's international partnerships, with a focus on the Africa and India corridors.

SWOT.

Strengths and weaknesses come from inside TechBBQ; opportunities and threats from outside.

Strengths

Internal, positive

  • Scale: 10,000 participants, 900+ investors, 37 countries (2025)
  • The full investor ladder: Investor Day, LP Forum, Pension Summit, Family Office Summit
  • A working corridor model: India summit in its third year, Africa in its second
  • Strong foundation backing: DKK 4m from Novo Nordisk Foundation
  • A proven partner model: the Danish Life Science Cluster's DKK 750,000 area
  • A measurement culture: Theory of Change and annual impact reports

Weaknesses

Internal, negative

  • Reliance on one foundation: the Africa and India corridors run largely on one grant
  • No paying African government agency yet (KeNIA aside)
  • Country pavilions aren't an established format: the event guide doesn't mention them
  • Most startup programmes focus on European startups

Opportunities

External, positive

  • India–Nordic Green Technology and Innovation Strategic Partnership (May 2026)
  • Denmark's 2024 Africa strategy and Impact Fund Denmark's DKK 350m Africa Facility
  • DI's new Danish-African Business Network (June 2026)
  • Nordic DFIs already invest in African tech funds (Norfund, Swedfund)
  • A growing Nordic ecosystem: $7.7bn in venture funding in 2025, on track for $9bn+ in 2026
  • Many countries already run pavilions at Slush; TechBBQ can be their second Nordic stop

Threats

External, negative

  • Competing events want the same country budgets: Slush, Web Summit, VivaTech, London Tech Week
  • In Africa: scarce early-stage capital, currency volatility, unclear exits
  • Visas: Indian startups need a Schengen visa and cover their own costs
  • Europe's tech sovereignty debate may make non-European capital a harder sell
  • The grant is time-limited: the corridors must move to partner revenue within three years

What the SWOT says to do.
